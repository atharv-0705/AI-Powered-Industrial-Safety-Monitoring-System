Hardware rule: camera capture can be 30 FPS, but the AI workload is intentionally scheduled at lower rates. Suggested starting point: YOLO 10–15 FPS, pose 10–15 FPS, HAR 5–10 FPS, and VLM only after an event trigger.

MVP Safety Events

Three events are enough to demonstrate the complete architecture.

⌁

Fall Detection

Signal: pose + temporal motion features. Detect rapid downward movement, body orientation change and low-position persistence.

●

No Helmet

Signal: PPE detector + person association. Avoid asking the VLM to perform the low-level helmet decision.

□

Restricted Zone

Signal: person tracking + polygon geometry + temporal persistence. No additional deep model is necessary.
